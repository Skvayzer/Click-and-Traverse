## Ladder and interpretation

Rungs are exactly 0.20, 0.15, 0.10, 0.07, 0.05 m (`cat_mjlab/tolerance_curriculum.py:8`). The final criterion remains 5 cm throughout public success telemetry and reset certification. Only the separate curriculum posture-success gate and within-role sampling feedback use the episode's current tolerance (`cat_mjlab/task.py:463`, `cat_mjlab/task.py:519`). The continuous region penalty and heading objective do not depend on metric tolerance and are unchanged. This is a success/sampling curriculum, not a new dense reward or geometric width curriculum; its learning benefit is unverified.

The supplied historical mean XY box error is approximately .388 m (also documented in `docs/V5_RESET_FIX_20260921.md:51`). At region scale .15, inverting c=d²/(d²+.15²) for the supplied .768 cost gives an equivalent single-distance .273 m. Inverting an averaged nonlinear, phase-weighted cost does NOT recover mean distance or a distance distribution. We have no measured quantiles supporting a quantile-calibrated ladder. A 20 cm start is below both summaries: forgiving enough to reward meaningful approach in the success gate, but not so loose as 40 cm that typical distant hands could qualify. Subsequent reductions of 5, 5, 3, 2 cm taper the difficulty jump. The certified seeded poses already have zero box error/region cost (48 poses, 12 scenes; `docs/V5_RESET_FIX_20260921.md:7`), so they cannot establish which rung is learnable from an ordinary reset.

Advance at >=60% on the latest 256 resolved unseeded leader episodes **for each of protected and transition roles separately** (`cat_mjlab/tolerance_curriculum.py:35`). A success requires clean goal, required-zone observation, heading compliance and tolerance compliance during >=90% of observed core-zone steps. Failures and timeouts are counted; unresolved episodes cannot create successes. Seeded successes are excluded from advancement. This is deliberately stricter than a pooled gate: easy protected results cannot mask transition failure. 256 samples give roughly 3 percentage points binomial standard error near 60%, an engineering choice rather than an independence guarantee for parallel RL episodes. It is large enough to resist tiny bursts and small enough to refresh after learning changes. The existing width implementation uses accumulated counts, not a rolling window; this implementation explicitly rolls and clears evidence on advancement.

A rung is frozen per episode at reset. Old-rung completions cannot advance a newer rung, and one update cannot skip rungs. Resume checkpoints retain the windows, per-rung totals, episode rungs and zone counts (`cat_mjlab/task.py:801`). Fresh launches require explicit `--hand-tolerance-curriculum`; omission preserves prior behavior, including inherited checkpoints. Threshold uses existing `--hand-curriculum-success-threshold`; the prepared command explicitly sets .60 because the source run recorded .35. Logs expose `hand_tolerance/rung`, `tolerance_m`, and per-rung/per-role completed, successes and rolling_success.

## Checks and remaining risk

CPU batches: 42 passed in 33.30 s; 23 passed in 19.44 s (four curriculum tests overlap). Coverage includes actual task outcome wiring, strict-vs-loose reward invariance, default no-op, per-role rolling eviction, seeded exclusion, stale-rung exclusion, final-rung cap, task checkpoint roundtrip, compiled reward kernel on CPU, prior width behavior, raised-reset certificates and bank validation. Test details and live measurements: [checks.json](assets/hand-tolerance-v6-20260922/checks.json). No GPU simulation or learning effectiveness test was run.

I support the ladder as a controlled aid, but 80% in-zone seeding risks learning only to maintain a reset pose, with poor upstream acquisition and collapse under motion. Requiring unseeded success to advance prevents seeded-only apparent mastery; it may also leave the ladder at 20 cm if transfer never happens. At 45% hand resets, 24% of all resets are seeded protected, 6% unseeded protected and 15% transition. Tightening alone cannot prove generalization; strict unseeded passage acceptance and seeded-after-100-step compliance should be watched. The live run at 4,915,200 steps had zero seeded-after-100 compliance over 95,632 active samples, and unseeded region cost .761581. Those measurements support concern about pose retention, not a conclusion about eventual training.

The live run's run.json records max_action_std=.15, contrary to the requested description of it as uncapped. Nothing was changed in that run; the prepared command explicitly disables the cap with 0. Existing flat/retention-based best.pt selection is also unchanged and does not select directly for hand protection (`cat_mjlab/runner.py:581`). Thus best.pt alone is not evidence of best hand behavior.

Live metrics grew from 226,303 to 284,736 bytes during the bank audit and reached 4,915,200 steps at the subsequent check. No signals, tmux commands altering state, GPU commands, or live-output writes were issued. Direct tmux inspection was denied by the sandbox, so full process-health verification is unavailable; continued output progress was observed.
